Credit Risk Workflow Tool - Project Requirements

1. Executive Summary:
- Develop a workflow management tool for credit limit approvals, tracking requests from initiation through approval stages.
- Improve visibility, efficiency, and reduce processing time.

2. Business Objectives:
- Streamline workflow, provide transparency, reduce approval time, improve coordination, centralize documentation, generate metrics, and plan for future system integrations and AI enhancements.

3. Core Features (Version 1):
- Workflow tracking for credit limit requests
- Digital forms for submissions and approvals
- Dashboard for status tracking and reporting
- Document storage and retrieval
- Priority management for requests

4. User Roles:
- Credit Analyst: Reviews and analyzes requests
- Relationship Manager: Submits requests
- Legal Reviewer: Reviews legal docs
- Credit Approver: Approves limits
- Committee Approver: For high-risk approvals
- Business Sponsor: Senior stakeholder
- System Administrator: Manages settings and access

5. Workflow Management:
- Parent process: Credit Paper (approval/rejection)
- Sub-processes: Credit Request, Credit Review, Business Sponsorship, Questionnaire, Legal Review, Credit Analysis, Compilation, Approval
- Each sub-process has draft, in-progress, submitted, and sometimes rejected states
- State transitions and audit trails required

6. Architecture:
- Backend: Django, DRF, PostgreSQL
- Frontend: React, Material-UI
- Modular Django apps: users, workflow_engine, credit_applications, documents, core
- Service layer for business logic
- Role-based permissions, JWT auth
- File storage: local (dev), cloud (prod)

7. Database/Entities:
- User, Department, Role, Permission
- WorkflowDefinition, State, Transition, WorkflowInstance, StateLog
- CreditApplication, Counterparty, LimitRequest, Document, DocumentType
- Generic relations for flexible attachment of workflows and documents

8. Document Management:
- Attach documents to applications via ContentType
- Define document types, allowed extensions, size limits
- Unique storage paths, preserve filenames

9. Design & UI:
- Dashboard, digital forms, reporting, clear status indicators
- Role-based field visibility and options
- Color palette and typography as per design brief

10. Constraints:
- Open-source preference, 2-3 requests/week expected

11. Future Enhancements:
- Integration with external systems
- Advanced analytics, automated doc generation, AI-assisted data entry, notification system

12. Project Timeline:
- Development: 2 months (timely delivery preferred)

13. Non-Functional:
- Security, performance, scalability, maintainability, test coverage, auditability

14. Out of Scope (for v1):
- Full external system integration, advanced analytics, AI features
